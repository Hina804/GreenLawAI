Issue
Whether the unauthorized extraction, transportation, and sale of timber from a designated Reserved Forest in Mansehra District constitutes a criminal offence under the KP Forest Ordinance 2002 and the Forest Act 1927, and what penalties apply to the accused persons.
Rule
Under Section 37, KP Forest Ordinance 2002, any person who cuts, fells, girdles, lops, taps, or strips bark/leaves from trees in a Reserved Forest without authorization commits an offence punishable by imprisonment up to 3 years and/or a fine of ₨50,000–₨500,000. Section 26 of the Forest Act 1927 additionally prohibits trespassing in reserved forests and offences thereunder carry penalties of ₨500 fine per tree. PEPA 1997 Section 14 further empowers environmental tribunals to impose restoration orders.
Application
Based on satellite evidence from NASA FIRMS and GFW GLAD data showing 3.2 ha of cleared Reserved Forest area in Mansehra Block-7, combined with field patrol reports confirming timber loading operations, the facts constitute unlawful extraction. The accused were operating without a timber transit permit under Section 41 of the KP Forest Ordinance 2002. AI threat confidence at 92% corroborates physical evidence. Multiple accused persons qualify for joint prosecution under Section 34 PPC (common intention).
Conclusion
The extraction activity constitutes a cognizable offence under KP Forest Ordinance 2002 §37 read with Forest Act 1927 §26. Recommended action: (1) FIR registration under §37 KP Ordinance, (2) confiscation of timber and vehicles under §39, (3) application for ₨250,000 fine plus restoration order, (4) referral to Environmental Tribunal for PEPA 1997 proceedings.
